# Gym SaaS

A multi-tenant platform where a gym owner runs one or more gyms, and members hold access to a gym for a paid period.

## Language

**Owner Subscription**:
A gym owner's paid access to the platform.
_Avoid_: Member Subscription, gym membership, plan

**Member Subscription**:
A member's paid access to a gym for a period. The gym records the payment itself.
_Avoid_: Owner Subscription, platform plan

## Notices

**Device push**:
A banner on a phone or computer where that person has turned notifications on.
_Avoid_: notification, alert, in-app notice

**In-app notice**:
An item in the notifications list inside the app.
_Avoid_: device push, email

**Announcement**:
A message a Super Admin publishes to gym owners, members, or both.
_Avoid_: reminder, subscription notice
